# CV.md — `/cv`

## Goal

Make recruiter scanning extremely fast while preserving the portfolio's design character.

This page prioritizes information density over visual spectacle.

---

## 1. Header block

Include:

- `Curriculum Vitae` or `CV`
- positioning title
- concise professional summary
- `Download PDF`
- Contact
- LinkedIn/GitHub when known

---

## 2. Desktop structure

Use a 7/5 or 8/4 editorial split.

Main column:

- Experience
- Selected projects
- Education

Side column:

- Selected skills/capabilities
- languages
- links
- compact contact

Use thin vertical rules and whitespace.

---

## 3. Experience

For each verified role:

- dates
- role
- employer/company
- concise scope
- 2–4 concrete impact/responsibility bullets

No invented data.

---

## 4. Skills

Use grouped capabilities, not huge keyword clouds.

Avoid proficiency percentages.

---

## 5. Print/PDF behavior

The web CV should have a print stylesheet.

PDF download can later point to a separately maintained PDF or generated version.

Print rules:

- white/paper background
- no navigation/footer clutter
- preserve readable typography
- avoid content being cut across pages when possible

---

## 6. Mobile

Single-column, recruiter-first order:

Summary → Experience → Projects → Skills → Education → Links.
