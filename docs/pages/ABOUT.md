# ABOUT.md — `/about`

## Goal

Explain Martin's professional trajectory and way of thinking without turning the page into autobiography or generic personal-brand copy.

---

## 1. Header

- eyebrow: `About`
- strong editorial title around systems, products and people
- concise introductory paragraph

Optional portrait later, but layout must work without one.

---

## 2. Narrative

Use 3–5 compact sections rather than one giant biography.

Suggested themes:

- engineering foundation
- firmware/software background
- move toward product/system-level work
- AI as leverage rather than identity
- psychology/human-centered thinking where appropriate and accurate

Keep claims defensible.

---

## 3. Working principles

A small editorial list can show how Martin works.

Examples of themes:

- understand the problem before the implementation
- use automation to buy back thinking time
- treat UX as system behavior, not decoration
- test uncertain assumptions cheaply
- prefer explicit tradeoffs over accidental complexity

These are principles, not motivational slogans.

---

## 4. Timeline / experience bridge

Can show a concise timeline using verified data only.

If employment details are not populated yet, hide the section or keep data in development fixtures.

---

## 5. Capabilities bridge

Link back to work/case studies rather than duplicating a long skills list.

---

## 6. Visual treatment

Use warm paper, large editorial typography and one meaningful diagram/image at most.

No lifestyle-photo collage.
No personality-test aesthetic.
No giant quote from Martin.
