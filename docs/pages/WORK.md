# WORK.md — `/work`

## Goal

Present Martin's work as systems/case studies, not a gallery of pretty thumbnails.

---

## 1. Intro

- page index/eyebrow
- display title: `Selected Work` or `Work`
- short statement explaining that public and private projects can both be documented through architecture, decisions, screenshots and demos

No oversized marketing paragraph.

---

## 2. Filtering

Only add filters when enough projects exist to justify them.

Potential filters:

- All
- AI Systems
- Product
- Engineering
- Automation
- Experiments

Do not show a filter bar for 2–3 projects.

---

## 3. Project listing

Prefer an editorial alternating layout:

- large visual + text
- next project with inverse alignment
- occasional compact row for smaller projects

Each entry may include:

- project title
- summary
- year
- role
- visibility: public/private
- 2–4 meaningful technology/category tags
- live/demo/GitHub links when available
- case-study link

Real project visuals dominate.

---

## 4. Private projects

Use explicit but neutral language:

`Private source` or `Repository private`.

Still show allowed evidence:

- architecture
- product decisions
- screenshots
- video
- live site
- lessons

Do not apologize for private repositories.

---

## 5. Responsive

Mobile becomes a deliberate single-column sequence with full-width visuals and concise metadata.

Do not retain alternating left/right alignment mechanically on mobile.
